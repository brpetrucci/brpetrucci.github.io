Hello! Life has been crazy and I haven't posted anything here, but figured I should post about the talk I gave almost a month ago!!

The folks over at [Palaeoverse](https://palaeoverse.org) were very kind to invite me to give a talk about my work, and I decided to use this opportunity to bring more attention to the stratigraphic-range fossilized birth-death (SRFBD) model, which I believe is the future of paleo-phylogenetics!! Check out my [Research](https://brpetrucci.github.io/research) for more details on this project. 

In my talk, I discuss the results of my simulation study comparing SRFBD with more traditional FBD models, and some interesting aspects of my empirical application of the model to a dataset of Canidae. Then, I walk the viewers through a tutorial--which you can also check out on this very website [here](https://brpetrucci.github.io/tutorials/srfbd)! I hope my talk and tutorial will make others excited to check this model out, and I'm always here to answer any questions about it!

If you want to watch the talk, it's on the [Palaeoverse YouTube channel](https://youtu.be/JBfijmmg73Q?si=vkJEBfCueCxpShc1). Hope you enjoy it!

I'll try and use this page to share more news in the future. With my defense coming up and (hopefully!) a new job in the horizon, I'm sure I'll have much to say.

Thanks for reading! Até mais!!
